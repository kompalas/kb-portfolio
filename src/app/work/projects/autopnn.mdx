## Overview

At the University of Patras, I led the AutoPNN project from 2024 to 2025. The project developed an automated hardware/software framework for co-designing edge AI accelerators and ultra resource-efficient machine learning models for flexible electronics.

The framework included differentiable feature selection and variation-robust training.
