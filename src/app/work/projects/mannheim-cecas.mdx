## Overview

At Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), I worked on MANNHEIM-CeCaS from 2021 to 2024. The work co-designed a machine learning hardware accelerator and trained vision, segmentation, and detection models for sensor-side AI tasks.

It also developed an ML scheduling algorithm to distribute tasks between a car server and an edge SoC, and contributed to automotive-grade design feasibility with industry partners including Bosch, Continental, and Infineon.
