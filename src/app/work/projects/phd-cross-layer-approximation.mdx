## Overview

My PhD research at KIT ran from 2020 to 2024 and focused on cross-layer approximation techniques for the synthesis and optimization of machine learning accelerators.

The dissertation was titled "Cross-Layer Approximation Techniques for Synthesis and Optimization of Machine Learning Accelerators". The PhD was jointly affiliated with the Physics Department at Aristotle University of Thessaloniki (AUTH).
